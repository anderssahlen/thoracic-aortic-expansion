# thoracic-aortic-expansion
JavaScript applet illustrating modeling findings published in the article Impact of Gender and Race on Allometric Indexing of Thoracic Aortic Size: Longitudinal Analysis of Multi-Ethnic Data from a Tertiary Cardiac Hospital by Sahlén JAPSE 2026. In brief, we studied n = 9,887 patients who had undergone 2 or more echocardiograms during a period of at least 12 months under follow-up at a large tertiary cardiac hospital. 

At our centre, approximately 14% of out-patients are found to have thoracic aortic ectasia, and our aim in this project was to understand how the expansion behaves over time. Earlier results obtained in a subset of the population (see Ng Heliyon 2023;9(5):e15823) had suggested that the expansion of thoracic aortic size is faster with larger size but slower in the elderly. Since this creates two opposing forces, we expanded the population in the present article and modeled the expansion in more detail. 

Of note, the population under study contains patients with normal thoracic aortic size or ectasia on echocardiography. Syndromic patients have been excluded and as the 99th percentile for size was 45 mm, very few patients in this population had aneurysmal aortas. 

Also note the disclaimer at the bottom of the applet. We did not create this applet to enable you to make management decisions. The external validity of our findings has yet to be determined and the applet is an illustration of the findings in the article.
